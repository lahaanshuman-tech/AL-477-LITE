# AL-477 Lite Maintainer's Manifesto

This document defines the authority, vision, and governance of the AL‑477 Lite project.

---

## 👑 Authority
- Maintainer: **Anshuman**
- All ports (Python, Java, Rust, Go, C, etc.) are controlled and approved by the maintainer.
- No changes are considered official until merged by the maintainer.

---

## 🌐 Vision
AL‑477 Lite exists as:
- An **educational encoder/decoder** project.
- A demonstration of algorithm design, documentation discipline, and multi‑language portability.
- A showcase of curiosity, polish, and professional standards.

---

## 📑 Governance
- **SPEC.md** defines the algorithm flow.  
- **PORTING.md** ensures consistency across languages.  
- **LICENSE.md** protects ownership and restricts misuse.  
- **CONTRIBUTING.md** guides collaborators.  
- **CHANGELOG.md** records official updates.  

All these documents are subordinate to the **Manifesto**.

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
AL‑477 Lite is **not a secure cryptographic algorithm**.  
It is for **educational purposes only**.  
STD and PRO versions remain proprietary and are not open for contribution.

---

## ✅ Maintainer’s Principles
1. **Clarity**: Documentation first, code second.  
2. **Consistency**: All ports follow the same algorithmic flow.  
3. **Control**: Only the maintainer decides what is official.  
4. **Curiosity**: The project evolves through exploration and polish.
